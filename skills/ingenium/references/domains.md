# Domain boundaries

Keep the creative method portable. Load domain facts and tools only when the selected output needs them.

**Screens, editorial work and objects:** use representative content and evaluate the intended reading or interaction task at actual sizes. Make clear whether you've produced a visual study or a usable implementation.

**Physical objects and spaces:** leave unknown dimensions, loads, material properties and site facts open, and ask for the consequential ones as they become relevant. Keep proposed values labeled as proposals. A compelling appearance doesn't establish stability, manufacturability or construction adequacy.

**Formal engineering views:** preserve the authoritative model, notation, source versions, IDs and declared relationships, and use the project's semantic rendering path. Never infer a connection from a line crossing, and never let a visual treatment change a port, quantity, obligation or dependency. Ingenium contributes exploratory presentation decisions; the model and the engineer own meaning and acceptance.
