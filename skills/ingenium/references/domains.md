# Domain boundaries

Keep the creative method portable. Load domain facts and tools only when the selected output needs them.

For screens, editorial work and objects, use representative content and evaluate the intended reading or interaction task at actual sizes. Distinguish a visual study from a usable implementation.

For physical objects and spaces, leave unknown dimensions, loads, material properties and site facts open. A compelling appearance does not establish stability, manufacturability or construction adequacy. Ask for consequential facts as they become relevant, and preserve proposed values as proposals.

For formal engineering views, preserve the authoritative model, notation, source versions, IDs and declared relationships. Use the project's semantic rendering path. Never invent a connection from a line crossing, or let a visual treatment change a port, quantity, obligation or dependency. Ingenium supplies exploratory presentation decisions; the model and engineer retain meaning and acceptance.
