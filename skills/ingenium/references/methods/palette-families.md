# Palette families

**Question:** Which coordinated color roles support the content and intended tone?

**Make:** A few families using the same role names, the full semantic role map and the identical applied specimen. Separate canvas, surface, text, accent and semantic feedback where those roles exist. Keep semantic meaning stable across families and give status a second cue (label or shape) beside color. Declare which foreground/background pairs are allowed and which combinations are unqualified. Record actual color values.

**Review it for:** Inspect the swatches and the applied content together. Measure the relevant text/background pairs with the [scoped checks](../checks.md), within their composition limits. Labels like "warm" or "restrained" are interpretations, not scores.

**Done when:** Each family is shown on the same specimen and the measured pairs are recorded. Treat unmeasured combinations as not checked rather than claiming a palette-wide pass. One contrast pass doesn't cover every state, transparency or color-vision need, and focus ink measured against a surface doesn't qualify a ring beside a dark action, its geometry, or the focus/selection distinction. The example palette is a study choice, not Ingenium's identity.

Use the [shared study contract](contract.md). Consequential interactions also use [connected sketches](../interaction-sketches.md).
