# Structural wireframe

**Question:** Can the person find the relevant information and next action?

Use restrained shapes and actual text to expose hierarchy, grouping, navigation, controls and supporting context. Distinguish primary content, editable inputs and annotations. Include a realistic long label or dense item where it matters. Label hidden or collapsed information rather than implying it does not exist.

**Review:** Ask the person to locate a specific item and describe the likely next action. Check that the intended hierarchy is visible at delivery size. Geometry does not prove task completion, responsiveness or accessibility; a static button has no executed behavior.


Record target viewport and map visible actions to their source or proposed interaction requirement. Probe realistic long labels and dense content; inspect resizing separately. A static fixed viewport does not establish responsive behavior.
