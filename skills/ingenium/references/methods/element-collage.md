# Element collage

**Question:** Could this visual vocabulary support the intended experience?

**Make:** A small original collection of type, color, shape, line, surface and component fragments, with enough applied content to reveal how they relate. Explain the proposed organizing idea and declare which treatment variables may change and which task content stays fixed. A collage needn't look like a finished page or imply an information architecture.

For a controlled local comparison, use two or three fragments with identical wording per row, and state the constants, variables, question, tradeoff and open choice. Add a small compatibility strip showing the selected fragments can coexist. Keep wider exploration separate from that controlled fragment: changing several factors at once doesn't isolate a cause or pick a winner. (The Relay example pairs filled and outlined badges inside an exploratory collage, with no winner selected.)

**Review it for:** Ask which relationship or fragment supports the purpose and which distracts. Keep reactions tentative and scoped to what was compared.

**Done when:** The organizing idea, variables and held content are stated and the fragments are inspected at delivery size. Selected external assets keep their provenance; permission to inspect a reference isn't a right to copy it. A collage suggests direction; it doesn't establish grouping, behavior or a universal brand identity.
