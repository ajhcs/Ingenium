# Element collage

**Question:** Could this visual vocabulary support the intended experience?

Assemble a small original collection of type, color, shape, line, surface and component fragments with enough applied content to reveal relationships. Explain the proposed organizing idea and declare which treatment variables may change versus which task content stays fixed. Exploration may vary type, color, shape and surface together, but that does not isolate a cause or establish a winner. A collage need not resemble a finished page or imply an information architecture. Keep resource provenance with selected external assets; permission to inspect a reference does not grant rights to copy it.

**Review:** Ask which relationship or fragment supports the purpose and which distracts. Keep reactions tentative and scoped. A collage suggests visual direction; it does not establish semantic grouping, interaction behavior or a universal brand identity.


For a local controlled comparison, use two or three fragments with identical wording/content per row; declare constants, variables, question, tradeoff and open choice. Add a small compatibility strip showing that selected fragments can coexist. Keep wider exploratory assembly distinct from that controlled fragment; changing several factors together does not isolate a cause. The Relay example pairs filled/outlined badges inside an exploratory collage, with no selected winner.
