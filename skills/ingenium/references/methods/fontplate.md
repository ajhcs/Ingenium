# Fontplate / type specimen

**Question:** Which type roles make this content readable and appropriately expressive?

Use comparable real text at actual delivery sizes: headings, body, labels, numerals and ambiguous characters as relevant. Record requested family, weight, size, line height and fallback stack. Separate two comparison modes. In a fixed-metrics family comparison, hold text, width, size, weight, line height and spacing steady, while noting that equal nominal values do not guarantee equal perceived size. In a tuned-fit comparison, permit and record per-family adjustments to size, line height or spacing; assess the resulting treatment without attributing the outcome to family alone. Vary role hierarchy separately when that is the question. A single specimen does not establish one family’s superiority. Include relevant language and glyph coverage rather than implying a small alphabet proves multilingual support.

**Review:** Inspect wrapping, density and character distinctions at intended size. Record the font actually loaded when the renderer can establish it; otherwise label rendering/fallback uncertainty. A family name in SVG is not an embedded font or proof of exact metrics. Keep font binaries, licensing and acquisition distinct from a specimen; use [resource routes](../resource-libraries.md) or [font tools](../fonts.md) only when those operations are requested.


Actually draw identical-copy fixed-metrics comparisons when comparing families. Include a realistic long label, numerals, 0O/1lI, units and relevant arrows/operators such as → ≤ Δ. Requested family and actual per-glyph rendered font are different evidence. If tuned-fit is omitted, say so; if included separately, show per-family metric deltas. A nominal match or small corpus does not establish all glyph coverage or family superiority.

Use the [shared study contract](contract.md). Consequential interactions also use [connected sketches](../interaction-sketches.md).
