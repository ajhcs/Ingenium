## Narrow synthesis and limits

This tranche adds corroboration rather than a new core workflow. It supports the already proposed optional presentation-context route (roles, scoped overrides, locale/content context) and meaning-first diagram route, while preserving existing eight methods and source-model authority. Fontplate already requires actual glyph/font evidence; icon/terminal cell advance and clipping are a tiny contextual addition, not a new typography system.

No upstream code, generated template, external connector, browser, pet generation, image API or separately billed operation was executed. No template or its declared license lineage was adopted. Source frontmatter and upstream MIT claims do not establish rights for every embedded font, artwork, mark or example. Product outcome benefits remain hypotheses; these reads are source coverage, not observed design evaluation or future-model compatibility evidence.
