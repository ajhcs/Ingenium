# Common Ground: two historical studies

`cover-v1.svg` is an earlier original synthetic study generated from the retained worked source. `cover-v3.svg` is the later V3 synthetic study. Both remain editable and embed their selected unchanged fonts. Complete font notices accompany this release.

These sources show different choices in typography, shape and color. They were not made as a controlled performance comparison. The labels identify revisions; they do not establish quality, usability or audience acceptance.
