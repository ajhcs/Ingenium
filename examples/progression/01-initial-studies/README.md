It is distinct from **Listening Garden**, the later event-poster progression. Small Hours' historical source and preview are retained in the private review archive; they are not newly redistributed here. The portable [Listening Garden generator](../../composition/listening-garden.mjs) represents the later measured-composition study. Small Hours' record describes author inspection and a successful export; Listening Garden's records include later specific feedback and scoped decisions. Neither establishes blanket acceptance or production quality. Exact historical review provenance is kept outside the public package.
