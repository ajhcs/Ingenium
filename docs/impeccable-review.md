# Selected Impeccable review practices

Ingenium now makes the order and reconciliation of review evidence explicit. A finding names its scene/state, evidence, observed defect or tentative consequence, smallest correction and preservation boundary. All eight methods and their existing source/model, editing, recovery and controlled-comparison contracts remain in place.

## Source and scope

The review freezes [Impeccable e3083ac3301e4d10517dd6cb58ab645bc2cb6e1b](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/tree/e3083ac3301e4d10517dd6cb58ab645bc2cb6e1b) against Ingenium 0ee17ff08da01f1aa6fdcb755799a33e71f364d9. Source/license research and Astra Max method recomposition were separate from integration. The actual eight baseline renders were inspected. No upstream scripts were executed.

The small adaptation comes from [critique](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/e3083ac3301e4d10517dd6cb58ab645bc2cb6e1b/skill/reference/critique.md) and [polish](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/e3083ac3301e4d10517dd6cb58ab645bc2cb6e1b/skill/reference/polish.md): record observations before automated summaries where practical, reconcile evidence, prioritize task failures, and repair the narrowest cause. Prior exposure is disclosed; this does not require an independent agent or a new approval step.

The review paragraph in the [shared contract](../skills/ingenium/references/methods/contract.md) is a modified adaptation under Apache-2.0. Its [complete license](../skills/ingenium/LICENSE-IMPECCABLE) and [attribution/change notice](../skills/ingenium/NOTICE-IMPECCABLE) travel with the portable skill, including the guidance ZIP. Relevant Paul Bakaus and historical Anthropic attribution is retained. No ehmo native-platform or Typecraft typography material is adapted.

## Disposition

| Piece | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Task observations before automated summaries; evidence tied to narrow corrections and preserved context | Adopted in one conditional review paragraph. |
| Product facts, surface intent and visual choices | Existing records already separate facts, interpretations and scoped decisions; no competing files or schema. |
| Real content, difficult states, retained work, actual rendering | Existing eight-method contracts retained; the example exercises the review boundary. |
| Useful desktop density, familiar affordances and simultaneous context | Preserved; fewer panes or choices are not assumed better. |
| Aesthetic bans, font popularity rules, aggregate scores, required issue counts and invented persona outcomes | Rejected as ungrounded acceptance criteria or scope changes. |
| Mandatory interviews, confirmation/delegation gates and concept/world selection rituals | Rejected as unnecessary process for authorized work. |
| Launcher, engine, detector, hooks, telemetry, comp pipeline and dependencies | Not imported; unnecessary for the selected practice. |
| Generic retry, rollback, authentication or submission semantics | Not substituted for actual source/product contracts. |

## Evidence and limits

The upstream [comp-fidelity report](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/e3083ac3301e4d10517dd6cb58ab645bc2cb6e1b/docs/COMP-FIDELITY.md) describes small paired experiments, higher costs and failed cases. It supports investigating that specific fidelity workflow; it does not establish general design superiority or improvement of Ingenium's methods.

The [controlled recovery example](../examples/review/README.md) is a newly authored prospective fixture, not an account of defects in earlier Relay studies. It compares error copy on identical content, geometry and state semantics. The review gallery and editable SVGs show the change and retained context. Its controls depict proposed product actions; they execute no edit/save/retry/Undo. Gallery disclosure and navigation checks are separate from native product behavior.

No user study, paired skill evaluation, task-performance gain, native binding, engineering acceptance, complete accessibility or installed-client qualification is claimed. See [validation](impeccable-validation.md) for the exact checks and omissions.
