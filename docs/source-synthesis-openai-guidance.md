# Official guidance and study assumptions

Retrieved 2026-10-21. The public Codex skill URLs redirect to ChatGPT Learn. Sources consulted:

- [Build skills](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/build-skills): focused capabilities, clear inputs/outputs, precise trigger descriptions, instructions before scripts unless deterministic behavior or external tools justify them, and progressive loading of metadata, SKILL.md, and supporting resources.
- [Reasoning best practices](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning-best-practices): concise direct goals, explicit constraints and success criteria, and avoiding requests to expose or prescribe internal reasoning.
- Local installed OpenAI skill-creator guidance: assume model competence, include non-obvious decision-changing information, match specificity to operational risk, and test observable outcomes. This local guidance is contextual evidence rather than a public versioned specification.

These sources support compact conditional references with goals, domain knowledge, invariants and verifiable outcomes. They do not establish that any proposed Ingenium addition improves current task performance or future intelligence scaling. That is our design hypothesis, subject to observed evaluation. They do not prescribe aesthetic bans, fixed research counts, mandatory thought scripts, new tool machinery or universal interaction patterns.

Research and implementation in this study use existing client agents. No separately billed API evaluation is authorized. Designed before/after examples demonstrate a distinction and are not controlled model-performance evidence. Controlled comparisons, if performed, must report the same task, model, reasoning setting, allowed context/tools, observations and limitations. A model critique alone does not establish a rendered or executed result.
