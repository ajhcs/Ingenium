## Limits

Correctness, award provenance, source reproducibility, preference and acceptance are distinct. This small study does not establish aesthetic superiority, causal effects of instructions/references, population preferences or representative-user task success. Print/physical-distance suitability, installed-client/editor behavior, complete accessibility, assistive technology and cross-browser behavior remain unqualified. No default instruction change, runtime/module/manifest/lock edit, broad library-suite rerun, merge, release, deployment or administration is included in this report-only follow-up. Historical reports remain truthful records of their own runs.
