The local store's flush/rename behavior is qualified on the exercised Linux filesystem, not against physical power loss or every operating system. Ambiguous foreign-process locks require investigation and are not stolen by age. Inspection executes a deliberately selected trusted local application's scripts; it is not a hostile-page sandbox. Its checks cover the visited routes, states, and browser environment, not general accessibility certification or backend behavior.

Resource availability, exact source identity, supported edits, rendered glyph identity, and qualification are separate fields. A catalog entry alone is not a qualified resource. Selected artwork is not a native engineering-model binding or an assertion of engineering adequacy. Font-dependent items with unresolved font closure remain unavailable for complete handoff qualification.

Keep failed attempts and unresolved output-specific findings with the relevant evidence. A repaired follow-up output does not change a failed initial evaluation into a first-run pass.
